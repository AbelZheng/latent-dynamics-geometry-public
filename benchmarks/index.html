Benchmarks

Benchmark Objects compare method applicability, robustness, and failure boundaries on predeclared synthetic tasks with known ground truth. Results are task-, metric-, and information-set-specific; this section is not a universal leaderboard.

Phase 4 status

Phase 4 is complete under the recorded review decision. P4-U01 through P4-U06 are independently reviewed L1/stable; P4-U07 passed two independent integration Red Teams and full milestone validation. Stable status is bounded by each object’s declared synthetic scenarios, targets, information sets, failure labels, and interpretation limits. Phase 5 has not begun.

  • P4-U01 — Linear-Gaussian Robustness Benchmark — bounded scalar LGSSM scenarios comparing simple references, filtering, smoothing, direct conditioning, and finite-grid selected inference. (L1, stable)
  • P4-U02 — Static Linear Latent Recovery Benchmark — bounded static rank-two scenarios comparing projection references, PCA, PPCA, diagonal-noise FA, and a known-parameter Gaussian reference with metric-specific provenance and non-pooled targets. (L1, stable)
  • P4-U03 — Temporal Covariance and Inference Benchmark — benchmark-local temporal-covariance implementation grounded in the stable Phase 3 design, comparing static and two-stage references, known/selected GPFA, wrong covariance priors, online Kalman filtering, retrospective smoothing, and applicability-limited Gaussian oracles. (L1, stable)
  • P4-U04 — Demixing and Rotational Projection Robustness Benchmark — separate demixing and rotational tasks with rank-gated marginal recovery, within-cell pseudo-population and label controls, selected covariance-matched rotational controls, target-specific failures and no cross-task score. (L1, stable)
  • P4-U05 — Nonlinear Reconstruction and Graph Geometry Robustness Benchmark — separate bounded nonlinear-reconstruction and finite graph/operator tasks covering curvature, Gaussian and support-shift controls, graph connectivity/shortcut/density/bandwidth/metric sensitivity, frozen held-out extension, and explicit non-manifold interpretation limits. (L1, stable)
  • P4-U06 — Cross-Suite Robustness Synthesis — stable-artifact-only inventory of seven Phase 4 tasks, reconciling source-local vocabularies, explicit vocabulary inventories, denominator-specific applicability/failure-label patterns and scenario-target coverage without importing metric values, pooling scores, ranking methods or naming a winner. (L1, stable)
