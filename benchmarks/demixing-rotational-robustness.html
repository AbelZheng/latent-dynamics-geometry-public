Demixing and Rotational Projection Robustness Benchmark

Demixing and Rotational Projection Robustness Benchmark

Scientific question

P4-U04 asks two separate synthetic questions.

  1. Label-dependent demixing: under known stimulus- and decision-dependent linear effects, when do condition means, PCA, marginal PCA, bounded dPCA and ridge decoding recover their own declared held-out targets, and how do those targets respond to collinearity, cell imbalance, label permutation, pseudo-populations, trial averaging and sample size?
  2. Rotational projection: under known rotational and non-rotational trajectory families, when do PCA planes, unrestricted derivative regression, skew-constrained jPCA-like fitting, symmetric fitting, input-aware fitting and fixed/random/control planes recover held-out derivative, plane, angular-rate, tangential-motion or covariance-control targets?

The two tasks have separate generators, scenario grids, methods, applicability rules, metrics, summaries and figure panels. No cross-task normalization, aggregate score, rank or winner is computed. Marginal reconstruction, label decoding, marginal-subspace recovery, rotational-plane recovery, angular-rate recovery and derivative prediction are also never combined into one score.

Ground truth

Demixing task

The demixing generator has eight observed features, 12 aligned time bins and a balanced binary stimulus \(s\in\{-1,1\}\) and decision \(d\in\{-1,1\}\) design in the reference scenario. A trial has clean signal

\[ y_{s,d}(t) = \mu(t) +0.9\,s\,f_s(t)v_s +0.75\,d\,f_d(t)v_d, \]

plus independent Gaussian observation noise with standard deviation \(0.42\). The reference \(v_s\) and \(v_d\) are orthonormal. The near-collinear scenario replaces the second direction by a normalized perturbation of the first. There is no interaction effect in this bounded generator. Trial shifts, feature-wise trial reassignment and label permutations are scenario operations, not hidden changes to the reference target.

The known targets are the stimulus and decision condition-mean marginalizations and their generating one-dimensional feature directions. Labels are observed supervision for fitting; clean signals and generating directions are unavailable to fitting and enter only scoring or verification.

Rotational task

The rotational generator has four latent coordinates, eight observed features, six conditions, 31 physical-time samples on \([0,1.2]\), and independent trials. The reference latent matrix contains a rate-four planar rotation and a second, slower damped rotational block. The primary plane/rate target is the first known observed-space plane and angular rate \(4\). Other scenarios replace the latent family with symmetric decay, latency sequences or input-driven trajectories, or alter the observation metric and preprocessing.

The observation map is an eight-by-four orthonormal embedding in ordinary scenarios and a declared nonorthogonal embedding in the metric stress. Gaussian latent and observation perturbations are added independently. Known latent states, embedding and generating matrices are scoring/verification truth only. They are not supplied to fitted projections.

Generation

Generation is task-specific and precedes all fitting.

  • Demixing: each scenario-replicate independently generates train, validation and test trials. Reference per-cell counts are 20/10/12, giving actual split totals 80/40/48. The small-sample per-cell counts 5/4/12 give totals 20/16/48; large-sample counts 45/18/18 give totals 180/72/72. The missing-cell training matrix is [[20,20],[20,0]], for total 60 and design rank three; validation/test remain balanced with totals 40/48. The unbalanced training matrix is [[40,5],[6,20]], for total 71 and design rank four; its validation/test totals are 40/48.
  • Rotation: each scenario-replicate independently generates 7/4/5 trials per condition for train/validation/test. Across six conditions, the actual totals are 42/24/30. Condition trajectories share the declared scenario family but receive independent perturbations.

Demixing condition means are arithmetic means within each observed cell. The complete-design marginalization then gives the four cells equal weight rather than weighting them by trial count. Rotational preprocessing first averages trials within each condition; when subtraction is enabled, it subtracts the across-condition mean separately at each time point.

The executable is benchmark-local. It records SHA-256 digests of the stable Phase 3 demixing and rotational-control executables as scientific-design provenance, but it does not claim to call or executable-reuse their numerical routines.

Negative controls and stress cases

Demixing controls include permuted labels, incomplete and unequal cells, within-cell pseudo-population reassignment and non-wrapping trial-alignment mismatch. The shuffled-dPCA control independently permutes both training and validation labels, reselects its own dPCA ridge parameter on the shuffled validation target, and then refits. Each shuffled decoder likewise independently reselects its own ridge parameter from shuffled training/validation labels. Thus shuffled controls do not inherit the lawful method’s selected parameter. A label permutation is expected to change supervised fitted states; it is not another independent replicate.

The pseudo-population operation permutes each feature’s complete trajectory only among trials in the same split and condition cell. Labels and cell means are preserved to numerical precision across all replicates (maximum discrepancy \(3.33\times10^{-16}\)). Across the three replicates, the off-diagonal trial-noise covariance change ranges from 0.0523–0.0644 in train, 0.0685–0.0766 in validation and 0.0690–0.0772 in test. It therefore isolates loss of simultaneous within-cell trial covariance rather than destroying condition means.

Rotational controls include symmetric dynamics, latency sequences, common input, short arcs, non-wrapping timing jitter, smoothing and subtraction alternatives, nonorthogonal metric distortion, a deterministic seed-derived random plane and a selected covariance control. The latter attempts 24 predeclared seed-derived proposals, each applying a cyclic condition shift separately to every PCA coordinate. A proposal is admissible only when at least two coordinate shifts are distinct and it materially changes cross-coordinate condition correspondence; a uniform all-coordinate shift is excluded as a global relabeling/no-op. Selection maximizes training covariance cosine similarity among admissible proposals only, with match threshold 0.97. Replicates 0/1/2 have 23/24/24 admissible proposals and select proposals 0/19/4 with similarities 0.999995, 0.999990 and 0.999985. All selected controls use multiple distinct shifts, materially alter cross-coordinate correspondence, and are labelled matched. Selection and matching occur on training scores; the control does not preserve condition pairing or establish dynamics.

No stress case is discarded because it weakens the preferred method, and no post-hoc scenario-specific parameter search is performed.

Replicates and deterministic seeds

Each of the 19 scenarios has three independent replicates. PCG64 seeds are deterministically derived from master seed 20260820, task, stable scenario hash, replicate, stage, split and, where needed, trial/control index. Methods within a replicate share the same generated data for paired comparison.

The q10, median and q90 summaries therefore use three replicate-level values. They are descriptive finite-sample summaries, not confidence intervals or stable tail estimates. Trials, conditions, cells and time points are not promoted to independent replicates.

Split discipline

Generation, fitting/selection, frozen recovery and scoring are separate stages.

  • Demixing fitting accepts only train/validation observations and labels. dPCA regularization and ridge-decoder regularization are selected without test data. Frozen recovery accepts fitted states and test observations/labels; truth is joined only in scoring.
  • Rotational preprocessing, PCA and derivative models are fitted from training observations and known training inputs where declared. Frozen recovery accepts fitted states, test observations and test inputs. Test latent states and the true embedding remain scoring-only.
  • Excluded test observations and truth cannot enter fitting by function signature. Poison diagnostics report zero fitted-state discrepancy for test exclusion and zero fixed-observation recovery discrepancy for truth poison.
  • Complete trials remain within one split. Adjacent time bins are never split across train and test.

The label- and condition-permutation controls are explicit alternate fitting paths, not leakage into the lawful path.

The unrestricted, skew, symmetric and input-aware models share the same training PCA coordinate system. Their derivative predictions are evaluated on held-out central finite differences. pca_plane is coordinates [0,1], whereas fixed_plane is the deliberately distinct coordinates [0,2]; they are not aliases. A retrospective condition-mean projection is not an online state estimator, and no online claim is made.

Parameter selection

For selected_dpca, the ridge grid is

\[ \lambda\in\{10^{-4},10^{-2},10^{-1},1\}. \]

Each candidate is fitted on training condition means and scored by the sum of validation stimulus- and decision-marginal mean-squared errors; ties use the smaller ordered \(\lambda\). Each ridge decoder uses the same grid with validation classification error and the same deterministic tie rule. The lawful and shuffled dPCA/decoder paths each perform their own selection on their own labels before their states are frozen. In the reference replicate used by the verifier, lawful/shuffled dPCA both select 1.0; lawful stimulus/decision decoders select \(10^{-4}/10^{-4}\), while shuffled decoders select \(10^{-4}/1.0\). These values are diagnostics for that replicate, not universal hyperparameters.

Rotational methods have no fitted hyperparameter search in this bounded implementation. Window, smoothing, subtraction, fixed/random planes and condition permutation are predeclared scenario or method choices, not selected from test performance.

Inference

Demixing recovery rebuilds held-out condition means and marginalizations from complete test trials. Training condition means, global/marginal PCA projectors, selected dPCA maps and shuffled-control maps are then applied according to their fixed roles. Trial decoders use three predeclared temporal averages per feature. The clean trial signal and generating directions are never inputs to recovery.

Rotational recovery applies training-fitted smoothing/subtraction statistics, feature mean and rank-four PCA basis to held-out condition means. Central finite differences define held-out derivative targets. Fitted derivative maps predict in the frozen PCA coordinate system. Plane methods project those same held-out scores. The input-aware model alone receives the declared held-out input sequence.

Scoring

Every results.csv row has task, scenario, replicate, seed, metric-specific provenance/split/evaluation stage, method family, information set, parameter source, target, status/reason, per-cell count arrays and actual split totals. Where relevant it also records selected hyperparameter, design rank, covariance-control match status and proposal index. The artifact contains only the following metrics.

Each metric is first aggregated across its declared held-out cells/trials, conditions or derivative pairs into one scenario-replicate-method value. diagnostics.json then reports q10, median, q90, applicability rate and failure rate across the three replicates for each task \(\times\) scenario \(\times\) method \(\times\) metric group.

Failure and applicability labels

The full Cartesian task-specific inventories are retained:

  • demixing: \(9\times3\times8\times7=1{,}512\) rows;
  • rotation: \(10\times3\times8\times5=1{,}200\) rows;
  • total: 2,712 unique canonical rows.

Applicability is resolved before numerical failure. Every row has one status:

  • ok: applicable and finite;
  • nonconvergence: linear-algebra failure in an applicable path;
  • invalid: malformed, missing or non-finite applicable score;
  • inapplicable: the method/scenario does not define the metric target.

Natural execution produced 1,041 ok, 1,617 inapplicable, 54 invalid and zero nonconvergence rows. By task, demixing has 567 ok, 891 inapplicable and 54 invalid; rotation has 474 ok and 726 inapplicable. All 54 natural invalid rows are the three missing-cell replicates’ otherwise applicable balanced marginal reconstruction/subspace combinations; the design rank is three of four. Every non-ok row has a machine-readable reason and no value. The absence of natural nonconvergence is not a robustness claim: real-path fault injection verifies both invalid and nonconvergence, while preserving inapplicable precedence.

Examples of inapplicability are decoder methods on marginal reconstruction, reconstruction methods on decoding, symmetric/input-aware fits on plane or rate targets, and plane/rate truth in non-rotational scenarios.

Interpretation limits

This object establishes only behavior under the declared generators, splits, scenario grids, targets, metrics and fitted information sets.

  • Demixing is label- and loss-dependent. It does not establish statistical independence, blind-source separation, unique latent causes, neural modules, anatomy, mechanism or causality.
  • Decoding accuracy is not representation, marginal reconstruction or demixing. A shuffled-label control is a supervision control, not a causal intervention on the data-generating system.
  • Missing/unbalanced cells and pseudo-populations change the estimand and available joint information. Finite output does not restore unidentified factorial effects or simultaneous noise structure.
  • Trial averaging and aligned smooth condition means can hide trial-level variability. They are not evidence for latent dynamics.
  • A rotational projection is not an oscillator, autonomous dynamical system, vector field or mechanism. Skew-constrained derivative fit describes a projection under declared preprocessing and metric.
  • A good plane angle does not imply good derivative prediction; high tangential ratio does not imply a true rotational generator; good derivative prediction does not imply autonomy or causality.
  • Latency sequences, common input, smoothing, subtraction, short windows, timing jitter and nonorthogonal coordinates can create, remove or distort rotational summaries.
  • No result establishes manifold structure, intrinsic dimension, biological validity, real-data transfer or universal method superiority.

# Demixing and Rotational Projection Robustness Benchmark

## Scientific question

P4-U04 asks two separate synthetic questions.

1. **Label-dependent demixing:** under known stimulus- and decision-dependent
   linear effects, when do condition means, PCA, marginal PCA, bounded dPCA and
   ridge decoding recover their own declared held-out targets, and how do those
   targets respond to collinearity, cell imbalance, label permutation,
   pseudo-populations, trial averaging and sample size?
2. **Rotational projection:** under known rotational and non-rotational

   tangential-motion or covariance-control targets?

aggregate score, rank or winner is computed. Marginal reconstruction, label
decoding, marginal-subspace recovery, rotational-plane recovery, angular-rate
recovery and derivative prediction are also never combined into one score.

## Generation

Generation is task-specific and precedes all fitting.

- **Demixing:** each scenario-replicate independently generates train,
  validation and test trials. Reference per-cell counts are 20/10/12, giving
  actual split totals 80/40/48. The small-sample per-cell counts 5/4/12 give
  totals 20/16/48; large-sample counts 45/18/18 give totals 180/72/72. The
  missing-cell training matrix is `[[20,20],[20,0]]`, for total 60 and design
  rank three; validation/test remain balanced with totals 40/48. The
  unbalanced training matrix is `[[40,5],[6,20]]`, for total 71 and design rank
  four; its validation/test totals are 40/48.
- **Rotation:** each scenario-replicate independently generates 7/4/5 trials
  per condition for train/validation/test. Across six conditions, the actual
  totals are 42/24/30. Condition trajectories share the declared scenario
  family but receive independent perturbations.

Demixing condition means are arithmetic means within each observed cell. The
complete-design marginalization then gives the four cells equal weight rather
than weighting them by trial count. Rotational preprocessing first averages
trials within each condition; when subtraction is enabled, it subtracts the
across-condition mean separately at each time point.

The executable is benchmark-local. It records SHA-256 digests of the stable
Phase 3 demixing and rotational-control executables as scientific-design
provenance, but it does not claim to call or executable-reuse their numerical
routines.

## Scenario grid

### Demixing scenarios

| Scenario | Declared manipulation | Primary boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `balanced_reference` | complete $2\times2$ design with orthogonal generating directions | bounded reference |
| `near_collinear` | decision direction nearly aligned with stimulus direction | target overlap and unstable separation |
| `missing_cell` | training counts `[[20,20],[20,0]]`; factorial design rank three of four | balanced marginal recovery is undefined |
| `unbalanced_cells` | training counts `[[40,5],[6,20]]`, with complete rank-four support | equal-cell marginalization versus unequal precision |
| `label_shuffle` | deterministic permutation of training labels before lawful fitting | supervision-destruction control |
| `pseudo_population` | within each split and condition cell, each feature's complete trial trajectory is independently permuted | cell means are preserved while simultaneous trial covariance changes |
| `averaging_mismatch` | independent trial-level shifts of $-1,0,+1$ bins with nearest-edge fill and no circular wrap | aligned condition means blur trial structure |
| `small_sample` | 5/4 train/validation trials per cell | estimation stress |
| `large_sample` | 45/18/18 trials per cell | higher-information comparison |

The grid is core-plus-stress, not a factorial isolation of individual causes.
The missing-cell training design has rank three rather than the required rank
four. The benchmark therefore reports the design-rank diagnostic and retains
held-out decoder scores, but labels every otherwise applicable balanced
marginal-reconstruction and marginal-subspace row `invalid` with reason
`incomplete_factorial_design_rank_3_of_4`. It does not use grand-mean filling to
claim identified balanced marginal recovery.

### Rotational scenarios

| Scenario | Declared manipulation | Primary boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `clean_rotation` | reference rotational generator and standard preprocessing | bounded positive reference |
| `symmetric_decay` | diagonal negative generator without a true rotational plane | structure-specific negative control |
| `latency_sequence` | sequential transient bumps rather than an autonomous rotation | latency-induced apparent sequence |
| `common_input` | input-driven state equation with known two-dimensional input | state-only versus input-aware prediction |
| `short_arcs` | first 12 time points only | limited angular occupancy |
| `time_jitter` | condition-dependent shifts $(-1,0,+1)$ applied to latent path and input with nearest-edge fill, never wrapping | alignment stress |
| `smoothed` | three-point moving average fitted/applied before projection | preprocessing dependence |
| `no_condition_subtraction` | omit across-condition time-mean subtraction | subtraction dependence |
| `nonorthogonal_metric` | nonorthogonal observation map | Euclidean metric dependence |
| `covariance_control` | select among admissible training-only feature-wise cyclic condition-shift proposals by covariance cosine; uniform global shifts are excluded | thresholded covariance-match control |

Plane and angular-rate truth are reported only for declared rotational
scenarios and are omitted for decay, latency, input and covariance-control
families where that target is not defined.

## Negative controls and stress cases

Demixing controls include permuted labels, incomplete and unequal cells,
within-cell pseudo-population reassignment and non-wrapping trial-alignment
mismatch. The shuffled-dPCA control independently permutes both training and
validation labels, reselects its own dPCA ridge parameter on the shuffled
validation target, and then refits. Each shuffled decoder likewise independently
reselects its own ridge parameter from shuffled training/validation labels.
Thus shuffled controls do not inherit the lawful method's selected parameter.
A label permutation is expected to change supervised fitted states; it is not
another independent replicate.

The pseudo-population operation permutes each feature's complete trajectory only
among trials in the same split and condition cell. Labels and cell means are
preserved to numerical precision across all replicates (maximum discrepancy
$3.33\times10^{-16}$). Across the three replicates, the off-diagonal
trial-noise covariance change ranges from 0.0523--0.0644 in train,
0.0685--0.0766 in validation and 0.0690--0.0772 in test. It therefore isolates
loss of simultaneous within-cell trial covariance rather than destroying
condition means.

Rotational controls include symmetric dynamics, latency sequences, common
input, short arcs, non-wrapping timing jitter, smoothing and subtraction
alternatives, nonorthogonal metric distortion, a deterministic seed-derived
random plane and a selected covariance control. The latter attempts 24
predeclared seed-derived proposals, each applying a cyclic condition shift
separately to every PCA coordinate. A proposal is admissible only when at least
two coordinate shifts are distinct and it materially changes cross-coordinate
condition correspondence; a uniform all-coordinate shift is excluded as a
global relabeling/no-op. Selection maximizes training covariance cosine
similarity among admissible proposals only, with match threshold 0.97.
Replicates 0/1/2 have 23/24/24 admissible proposals and select proposals
0/19/4 with similarities 0.999995, 0.999990 and 0.999985. All selected controls
use multiple distinct shifts, materially alter cross-coordinate correspondence,
and are labelled `matched`. Selection and matching occur on training scores;
the control does not preserve condition pairing or establish dynamics.

No stress case is discarded because it weakens the preferred method, and no
post-hoc scenario-specific parameter search is performed.

## Replicates and deterministic seeds

Each of the 19 scenarios has three independent replicates. PCG64 seeds are
deterministically derived from master seed `20260820`, task, stable scenario
hash, replicate, stage, split and, where needed, trial/control index. Methods
within a replicate share the same generated data for paired comparison.

The q10, median and q90 summaries therefore use three replicate-level values.
They are descriptive finite-sample summaries, not confidence intervals or
stable tail estimates. Trials, conditions, cells and time points are not
promoted to independent replicates.

## Split discipline

Generation, fitting/selection, frozen recovery and scoring are separate stages.

- Demixing fitting accepts only train/validation observations and labels.
  dPCA regularization and ridge-decoder regularization are selected without test
  data. Frozen recovery accepts fitted states and test observations/labels;
  truth is joined only in scoring.
- Rotational preprocessing, PCA and derivative models are fitted from training
  observations and known training inputs where declared. Frozen recovery accepts
  fitted states, test observations and test inputs. Test latent states and the
  true embedding remain scoring-only.
- Excluded test observations and truth cannot enter fitting by function
  signature. Poison diagnostics report zero fitted-state discrepancy for test
  exclusion and zero fixed-observation recovery discrepancy for truth poison.
- Complete trials remain within one split. Adjacent time bins are never split
  across train and test.

The label- and condition-permutation controls are explicit alternate fitting
paths, not leakage into the lawful path.

## Methods and information sets

### Demixing methods

| Method | Role | Parameter source | Recorded information set |
|---|---|---|---|
| `condition_mean` | training condition-mean marginal reference | train labels | supervised train |
| `pca` | rank-two global PCA projection of each marginal target | train fit | supervised train |
| `marginal_pca` | rank-one PCA fitted separately to each training marginal | train labels | supervised train |
| `selected_dpca` | ridge reduced-rank marginal map | train plus validation | supervised train, with `parameter_source=train_validation` |
| `ridge_decoder` | separate stimulus and decision trial classifier | train plus validation | supervised train/validation |
| `shuffled_dpca_control` | marginal map with independently selected ridge after deterministic train/validation-label permutations | permuted train/validation labels | permuted train/validation control |
| `shuffled_decoder_control` | decoder with independently selected ridge after deterministic train/validation-label permutations | permuted train/validation labels | permuted train/validation control |
| `factorial_design_diagnostic` | rank of the training design $[1,s,d,sd]$ | train labels | design diagnostic |

Reconstruction/subspace methods are not assigned decoding scores. Decoders are
not assigned marginal-reconstruction or marginal-subspace scores.

### Rotational methods

| Method | Role | Parameter source | Information set |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pca_plane` | first two coordinates of the training PCA representation | train fit | train-fitted projection |
| `unrestricted_fit` | unrestricted linear derivative regression; plane extracted from its skew part | train fit | train-fitted projection |
| `skew_fit` | skew-constrained derivative regression | train fit | train-fitted projection |
| `symmetric_fit` | symmetric derivative regression control | train fit | train-fitted projection |
| `input_aware_fit` | derivative regression on state and known input | train fit plus known input | train-fitted projection |
| `fixed_plane` | predeclared PCA-coordinate axes 0 and 2 | predeclared | fixed reference |
| `random_plane` | deterministic seed-derived rank-two plane | deterministic seed | train-fitted projection |
| `covariance_shuffle_control` | plane/control from the highest-similarity admissible training-only condition-shift proposal; uniform global shifts excluded | selected permuted conditions | selected permutation control |

The unrestricted, skew, symmetric and input-aware models share the same
training PCA coordinate system. Their derivative predictions are evaluated on
held-out central finite differences. `pca_plane` is coordinates `[0,1]`, whereas
`fixed_plane` is the deliberately distinct coordinates `[0,2]`; they are not
aliases. The verifier's maximum projector difference is 1.0, and their clean
plane-angle medians are 0.5389° and 89.6500°, respectively. A retrospective
condition-mean projection is not an online state estimator, and no online claim
is made.

## Parameter selection

For `selected_dpca`, the ridge grid is

$$
\lambda\in\{10^{-4},10^{-2},10^{-1},1\}.
$$

Each candidate is fitted on training condition means and scored by the sum of
validation stimulus- and decision-marginal mean-squared errors; ties use the
smaller ordered $\lambda$. Each ridge decoder uses the same grid with
validation classification error and the same deterministic tie rule. The
lawful and shuffled dPCA/decoder paths each perform their own selection on their
own labels before their states are frozen. In the reference replicate used by
the verifier, lawful/shuffled dPCA both select 1.0; lawful stimulus/decision
decoders select $10^{-4}/10^{-4}$, while shuffled decoders select
$10^{-4}/1.0$. These values are diagnostics for that replicate, not universal
hyperparameters.

Rotational methods have no fitted hyperparameter search in this bounded
implementation. Window, smoothing, subtraction, fixed/random planes and
condition permutation are predeclared scenario or method choices, not selected
from test performance.

## Inference

Demixing recovery rebuilds held-out condition means and marginalizations from
complete test trials. Training condition means, global/marginal PCA projectors,
selected dPCA maps and shuffled-control maps are then applied according to their
fixed roles. Trial decoders use three predeclared temporal averages per feature.
The clean trial signal and generating directions are never inputs to recovery.

Rotational recovery applies training-fitted smoothing/subtraction statistics,
feature mean and rank-four PCA basis to held-out condition means. Central
finite differences define held-out derivative targets. Fitted derivative maps
predict in the frozen PCA coordinate system. Plane methods project those same
held-out scores. The input-aware model alone receives the declared held-out
input sequence.

## Scoring

Every `results.csv` row has task, scenario, replicate, seed, metric-specific
provenance/split/evaluation stage, method family, information set, parameter
source, target, status/reason, per-cell count arrays and actual split totals.
Where relevant it also records selected hyperparameter, design rank,
covariance-control match status and proposal index. The artifact contains only
the following metrics.

### Demixing metrics

| Metric | Target | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `stimulus_marginal_nmse` | stimulus marginal reconstruction | held-out marginal reconstruction MSE divided by held-out stimulus-marginal mean square |
| `decision_marginal_nmse` | decision marginal reconstruction | analogous held-out decision score |
| `stimulus_subspace_angle_degrees` | stimulus marginal subspace | principal angle between the leading recovered stimulus-marginal direction and known $v_s$ |
| `decision_subspace_angle_degrees` | decision marginal subspace | analogous angle to known $v_d$ |
| `stimulus_decoding_accuracy` | stimulus label decoding | held-out complete-trial accuracy |
| `decision_decoding_accuracy` | decision label decoding | held-out complete-trial accuracy |
| `factorial_design_rank` | factorial-design identification | rank of the training design $[1,s,d,sd]$; it is a training diagnostic rather than a test score |

Marginal NMSE, subspace angle and decoding accuracy answer different questions.
A decoder can predict a label without reconstructing its condition-mean
marginal, and low marginal error does not establish single-trial decoding.

### Rotational metrics

| Metric | Target | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| `plane_angle_degrees` | rotational plane | observed-space principal angle to the first known rotational plane; only in applicable rotational scenarios |
| `angular_rate_abs_error` | angular rate | absolute error from the known first-plane rate $4$; only in applicable rotational scenarios |
| `derivative_rmse` | held-out derivative prediction | RMSE between predicted and central-difference held-out derivatives in training-PCA coordinates |
| `tangential_velocity_ratio` | plane tangential motion | norm of tangential projected velocity divided by total projected-velocity norm |
| `covariance_similarity` | condition covariance control | Frobenius-cosine similarity of flattened score covariance before and after condition permutation |

Derivative RMSE, plane angle, angular-rate error and tangential ratio are not
interchangeable evidence. In particular, tangential motion in a selected plane
can occur for transients or input-driven sequences.

Each metric is first aggregated across its declared held-out cells/trials,
conditions or derivative pairs into one scenario-replicate-method value.
`diagnostics.json` then reports q10, median, q90, applicability rate and failure
rate across the three replicates for each task $\times$ scenario $\times$
method $\times$ metric group.

## Failure and applicability labels

The full Cartesian task-specific inventories are retained:

- demixing: $9\times3\times8\times7=1{,}512$ rows;
- rotation: $10\times3\times8\times5=1{,}200$ rows;
- total: 2,712 unique canonical rows.

Applicability is resolved before numerical failure. Every row has one status:

- `ok`: applicable and finite;
- `nonconvergence`: linear-algebra failure in an applicable path;
- `invalid`: malformed, missing or non-finite applicable score;
- `inapplicable`: the method/scenario does not define the metric target.

Natural execution produced 1,041 `ok`, 1,617 `inapplicable`, 54 `invalid`
and zero `nonconvergence` rows. By task, demixing has 567 `ok`, 891
`inapplicable` and 54 `invalid`; rotation has 474 `ok` and 726 `inapplicable`.
All 54 natural invalid rows are the three missing-cell replicates' otherwise
applicable balanced marginal reconstruction/subspace combinations; the design
rank is three of four. Every non-`ok` row has a machine-readable reason and no
value. The absence of natural nonconvergence is not a robustness claim:
real-path fault injection verifies both `invalid` and `nonconvergence`, while
preserving `inapplicable` precedence.

Examples of inapplicability are decoder methods on marginal reconstruction,
reconstruction methods on decoding, symmetric/input-aware fits on plane or rate
targets, and plane/rate truth in non-rotational scenarios.

## Interpretation limits

This object establishes only behavior under the declared generators, splits,
scenario grids, targets, metrics and fitted information sets.

- **Demixing is label- and loss-dependent.** It does not establish statistical
  independence, blind-source separation, unique latent causes, neural modules,
  anatomy, mechanism or causality.
- Decoding accuracy is not representation, marginal reconstruction or
  demixing. A shuffled-label control is a supervision control, not a causal
  intervention on the data-generating system.
- Missing/unbalanced cells and pseudo-populations change the estimand and
  available joint information. Finite output does not restore unidentified
  factorial effects or simultaneous noise structure.
- Trial averaging and aligned smooth condition means can hide trial-level
  variability. They are not evidence for latent dynamics.
- **A rotational projection is not an oscillator, autonomous dynamical system,
  vector field or mechanism.** Skew-constrained derivative fit describes a
  projection under declared preprocessing and metric.
- A good plane angle does not imply good derivative prediction; high tangential
  ratio does not imply a true rotational generator; good derivative prediction
  does not imply autonomy or causality.
- Latency sequences, common input, smoothing, subtraction, short windows,
  timing jitter and nonorthogonal coordinates can create, remove or distort
  rotational summaries.
- No result establishes manifold structure, intrinsic dimension, biological
  validity, real-data transfer or universal method superiority.
